# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
A mix of corporate clients and executives, referring law firms seeking co-counsel, and high-net-worth individuals requiring specialized legal representation.

## Product Purpose
To serve as the digital portfolio and primary contact point for Satyendra Agarwal, an Advocate on Record. Success means establishing high credibility and converting visitors into consultation requests.

## Positioning
Specialized in Constitutional Law, Appellate Litigation, and Commercial Arbitration, combining the rigorous authority of a Supreme Court advocate with an approachable consultation process.

## Operating Context
Visitors are evaluating the advocate based on past precedent, legal expertise, and professionalism before committing to high-stakes legal proceedings.

## Capabilities and Constraints
The portfolio must maintain a high-end, "Lex Elegantia" aesthetic with a strict monochromatic theme while highlighting Landmark Matters (e.g., Supreme Court Civil Appeal Representation). 

## Brand Commitments
- Voice: Intellectual, disciplined, uncompromisingly professional, yet approachable for consultations.
- Identity Constraints: "Wireframe-Chic" with ultra-thin borders, sharp 0px corners, and generous negative space.
- Theming: Dual theme (Light/Dark) must maintain strict parity.

## Evidence on Hand
- Highlighted focus areas: Constitutional Law, Appellate Litigation, Commercial Arbitration.
- Demonstrated precedent: Supreme Court Civil Appeal Representation.

## Product Principles
1. **Clarity through Reduction**: The UI must recede to allow legal expertise to take center stage.
2. **Authoritative yet Approachable**: Balance Supreme Court prestige with an easy path to schedule consultations.
3. **Impeccable Craft**: Ensure every interaction, from theme toggles to mega-menus, feels premium and deliberate.
